Find the holes in your plot.

Paste your outline, one scene per line. For each scene, say whether it happens therefore or but, or just and then. Butwhy asks one question at a time about the weakest spot: the scene that only follows the last one, the antagonist who vanishes in the middle, the ending decided by luck.

  • Rules, not AI. Same answer every time, and you can see why it asked.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. No account, no upload.
  • Free and open source. Code on GitHub.
What it asks the classics

Questions, not verdicts. The classics break these rules on purpose; the point is to know when you do.

Start from your outline

One scene per line. Numbers and bullets are fine. Lines like “Act 1”, “Act 2”, “Act 3” (or “Beginning”, “Middle”, “End”) split the story into parts; without them, Butwhy guesses. A line that starts with “But” or “Therefore” keeps that link. This replaces the story on screen.

Butwhy runs entirely in your browser. No AI reads your story, no account; your story is saved only on this device. Built on the “but / therefore” rule of storytelling. Source on GitHub.
